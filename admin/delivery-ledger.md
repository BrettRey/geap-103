**Status:** Canonical instructor source for sequencing and assessment timing  
**Applies to:** 14-, 13-, and 12-content-meeting deliveries  
**Canonical assessment design:** `assessment-framework.md` v6

“Curricular Week” identifies a unit in the 14-unit course design. It does not always mean a separate calendar week. Dated section schedules map those units onto actual meetings.

## Invariants across delivery versions

| Evidence | Curricular timing | Invariant |
|---|---:|---|
| AI Decision Logs | Weeks 2–11 | Eight curated entries. Entries 1–2 and 3–5 receive formative feedback; entries 6–8 are summative. Round 2 feedback reaches students before the Week 11 unit. |
| Oral Micro-Defences | Weeks 4, 8, 11 | Three rounds with 3 → 5 → 7 criteria. Record Full/Partial/Deferred evidence. A 30-minute block cannot guarantee two full observations per student in classes of 15–24; do not score missing observation as weak performance. |
| Artifact Packages | Weeks 4, 10, 14 | Same requirements and weights: 7% + 8% + 10%. |
| Git Episodes | Practised from Week 6; due Week 12 | Three individual demonstrations: Restore, Error recovery, and Collaboration or a documented equivalent. |
| Oral Evidence-Indexed Reflection | Week 13 | Four-minute, two-CLO, one-to-one conversation using pre-tagged evidence. |
| Goal Document | Weeks 1–13 | Thirteen entries. In compressed delivery, entry 9 becomes pre-class planning and entry 10 remains protected class writing. |

The Decision Log window contains ten curricular weeks, Weeks 2–11. Older references to “8 of 12 eligible weeks” are incorrect.

## Delivery mappings

| 14 meetings | 13 meetings | 12 meetings |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–14 separately | Weeks 1–8; Weeks 9+10; Weeks 11–14 | Weeks 1–8; Weeks 9+10; Weeks 11–13; Week 14 asynchronous |

Do not combine Weeks 12 and 13. Week 12 supplies the evidence mapping, Git deadline, and oral rehearsal that make the Week 13 reflection valid and logistically possible.

## Make-up and workload controls

- A student who attended a Micro-Defence but was not observed sufficiently may book a short live follow-up outside class. Record Deferred until the observation occurs; this is not the absence make-up.
- A student absent for a Micro-Defence completes the same diagnostic and an ad hoc triad within seven calendar days. If more than two students need a make-up, schedule one shared make-up block rather than removing another lesson's circulation time.
- For the Monday section's October 5 Micro-Defence, schedule that live make-up by October 9; October 19 is outside the seven-day window.
- A student absent for the Week 13 oral reflection completes the same four-minute, two-CLO conversation within five instructional days. It may be in person or live online; it remains unmediated.
- Round 2 Decision Log feedback must arrive before the Week 11 unit. “Returned at the start of Week 11” is too late to guide entries 6–8.
- For classes above 20, publish the Week 13 reflection order in advance, require evidence pre-tagging before class, and reserve essentially the whole meeting for the reflection queue. Do not add another live graded assessment.
- Record package extensions and make-ups by date in the section LMS. Do not rely on week numbers alone.

## Access-neutral evidence rule

GitHub Education and Copilot Student are benefits, not prerequisites for graded evidence. Each student uses one declared path:

| Learning target | GitHub path | Equivalent path |
|---|---|---|
| Restore | Recover an earlier committed version | Recover an earlier local-Git, OneDrive, or other version-history state |
| Error recovery | Capture error, diagnosis, and fix | Capture an authentic or instructor-staged error in the student's available tool, then document diagnosis and fix |
| Collaboration | PR/change description plus comment exchange | Tracked change or version-history handoff plus the same what/why/what-not description and a response |

Watching an instructor demonstration or using a partner's history is practice, not individual assessment evidence.

## Operational contingency matrix

Use the response as soon as the trigger is visible. Do not consume the whole-class task window trying to restore the preferred tool.

| Point in sequence | Trigger | Immediate response | What must still be preserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 / late enrolment | Student has no OneDrive access, no device, or joins after the first meeting | Use a local Documents or campus-computer folder; issue the Week 1 catch-up sheet; make a dated backup before leaving | Folder map, course folder, Goal Document entry 1 |
| Week 2 AI cycle | No AI account, quota exhausted, blocked site, or model comparison cannot use two live tools | Give two prepared static responses to the same prompt; student evaluates the mismatch, revises the prompt, and documents the decision | Articulation → output → verification → decision cycle |
| Week 3 version history | GitHub registration, Education verification, Git installation, authentication, or network is blocked | After two focused attempts, move to local Git or document/cloud version history; continue account work later | Two named states and an actual usable recovery, produced by the student |
| Week 4 package | OneDrive share permission fails or folder is local-only | Accept Blackboard screenshots/file listing or an exported folder package plus an in-class spot-check | Logical structure, names, version/recovery evidence, process note |
| Week 4, 8, or 11 Micro-Defence | Student absent, triad breaks, instructor misses a presentation, or more than two make-ups accrue | Mark Full/Partial/Deferred; use a four-person group only if timing remains equal. A present student with insufficient direct evidence may book a short live follow-up outside class. An absent student completes the same diagnostic + ad hoc triad within seven days; consolidate 3+ cases in one block. | Same criteria, unmediated writing, oral explanation, recorded evidence basis |
| Week 5 accessibility | Word/Copilot feature differs or Accessibility Checker is unavailable | Use the prepared before/after files and a manual headings/alt-text/contrast checklist; verify directly rather than inventing a tool result | Identified issue, attempted fix, verification note |
| Week 6 spreadsheet | Shared link, Excel Online, or Copilot fails | After one quick fix, use downloadable `.xlsx` or CSV/HTML data; enter a formula manually or inspect prepared outputs | Interpretation, one independent calculation check, chart/data comparison |
| Week 7 collaboration | Fork/PR permission fails, instructor repo is locked, or partner is absent | Use an individual tracked-change/version-history copy and task-note template; form one triad or use the prepared sample response | Each student's change note, specific comment received, and reply |
| Week 8 restart | Low attendance after Reading Week or students cannot supply a genuine recent error | Re-form triads immediately; use instructor-staged errors downloadable from Blackboard | Same diagnostic and defence; authentic diagnosis language and help-request structure |
| Week 9 or compressed 9+10 | No draft, missing pre-class Goal entry 9, incompatible tool, or no after-class access | Adopt a starter artifact; write the task list first; reduce scope; preserve the last 20 minutes for packaging or arrange campus-lab time | Genuine audience/requirement, build, peer check, verification, honest documentation |
| Group formation / Week 11 | Student missed interest inventory, group member withdraws, or group has only one active member | Place ungrouped students before roles; merge undersized groups or create a pair with combined roles; keep individual contribution records | Named role, task record, recoverable individual contribution |
| Week 12 peer response | Share link or cross-group partner fails | Switch to Blackboard/file exchange; use one triad for odd numbers | One written response per student with visible change, evidence, and question |
| Week 13 reflection | Network/sign-in fails, student freezes, class exceeds 20, or student is absent | Use local/exported pre-tagged evidence; prompt from the Decision Log; publish the order; reserve essentially the full meeting; run the defined live make-up within five instructional days | Four live unmediated minutes, two CLOs, claims tied to evidence, follow-up answers |
| Week 14 live or async | Home technology, audio/video, accessibility, or gallery-posting barrier | Accept text, audio, or video; provide December 14 in-class completion or campus-lab access; collect through Blackboard if gallery fails | Same Final Package; one explanation; two specific peer responses; no production-polish penalty |
| Whole-class outage | Network or a shared platform fails | Continue with downloaded starter files, local copies, static AI outputs, and paper process tables; upload later | Language, evaluation, verification, and individual authorship evidence |

### Before each meeting

1. Open the lesson's public links while signed out.
2. Download the starter files and static examples to the classroom computer.
3. Confirm the Blackboard submission or exchange route.
4. Identify the assessment make-ups and partner/group absences already known.
5. Decide the maximum time for access troubleshooting before routing to an equivalent path.
6. Put the exact calendar date and deadline on the opening and closing slides; do not rely on “Week 10” alone.

## Term-setup checklist

- Confirm the registrar calendar and copy actual dates into the LMS.
- Publish only the schedule for the student's section.
- Test public course links and downloads while signed out.
- Test instructor-owned tools using the instructor's own account. Do not attempt to reproduce a student's individualized Education-verification decision.
- Collect only status categories and exact error wording, never passwords, authentication codes, identity documents, or enrolment documents.
- Tabletop class sizes of 15, 20, and 24 with an absence, a network outage, and at least 25% of students on non-Copilot paths.
