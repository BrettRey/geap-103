# GEAP 103 Decision Log

Keep this one file at `decision-logs/decision-log.md` in your course folder and, from Week 3 when available, your private portfolio repository. Add dated, numbered entries below. Use your own words and remove private information from copied excerpts.

For each entry, aim for about 150–200 words of your own writing, excluding copied AI excerpts and the self-assessment sentence. Record what you actually checked. If you used a prepared response instead of a live tool, say so.

Submit entries 1–2 for feedback in Week 4 and entries 3–5 in Week 8. Submit eight selected entries in Week 11. At each collection point, submit a permanent link to the committed file in Blackboard. If GitHub is unavailable, upload this `.md` file there instead. Your teacher returns assessment feedback and grades through Blackboard.

Copy the entry section below when you need another entry. Keep the entries you submit identifiable by number and date.

## Entry 1

**Date:**

**Task goal and constraints:** What did you want to do? Who was it for? What requirements mattered?

**Tool used:** Which tool did you use, and why this one? If you evaluated prepared answers, identify them here.

**Prompt snippets:** Copy up to three prompts. Leave out private information.

**Output summary:** What did the tool produce? Use one or two sentences.

**Decision:** Accept, Modify, or Reject. Explain why.

**Verification:** What did you check, and how? Point to a source, comparison, or test you actually used.

**What changed:** What did you change and why? If you kept the answer, explain why no change was needed.

**Self-assessment:** Excellent, Proficient, Developing, or Insufficient. Give one sentence explaining your rating.
