# GEAP 103 Week 14 asynchronous closeout

**Use with:** A 12-meeting section whose last live meeting is curricular Week 13  
**Fall 2026 Monday deadline:** Friday, December 18 at 11:59 p.m.  
**Expected student time:** 45–60 minutes, excluding final artifact work

This closeout replaces the live, ungraded Week 14 sharing event. It does not replace or alter the four-minute oral reflection in Week 13. The Final Artifact Package retains the standard 10% weight and requirements.

## Student instructions

### 1. Submit the Final Artifact Package

Include:

- the artifact;
- process documentation in your own words;
- a verification note stating what you checked, found, fixed, and did not finish; and
- at least one documented example of sharing or explaining the work.

Submit the strongest honest version you have. An incomplete feature named accurately in the verification note is better evidence than a claim that everything works.

### 2. Add your artifact to the class gallery

Choose one accessible format:

- a 1–2 minute audio explanation plus a link or image;
- a 1–2 minute captioned video;
- a short written explanation of approximately 100–150 words; or
- an in-person explanation completed during the final live class or campus-lab period.

Answer:

1. What did you build, and who is it for?
2. What changed between your plan and the result?
3. What is one decision you can explain clearly now?

Production polish is not graded. Do not include passwords, account screens, personal documents, private repository content, or another person's information.

### 3. Respond to two classmates

Write or record one specific response to each:

> I understood _____. I was interested in _____. My question is _____.

“Good job” by itself is not a complete response.

### 4. Complete the transfer reflection

> What is one thing you can do now that you could not do in the first class? Where will you use it in another course, at work, or in your life?

### 5. Complete the institutional course evaluation

Use the link provided in Blackboard. The instructor cannot see who wrote an individual response.

## Access branches

| Barrier | Alternative |
|---|---|
| No dependable home device or internet | Complete the package and gallery post before leaving Week 13 or during reserved campus-lab time |
| Audio/video is inaccessible or uncomfortable | Use the written gallery option |
| Writing is an accessibility barrier | Use audio or captioned video |
| Blackboard upload fails | Save a timestamped local copy and use the instructor's published backup submission route |
| No peer posts are visible | Respond to a provided worked example and one available peer; do not wait indefinitely |
| Student missed Week 13 oral reflection | Complete the separate live make-up by December 18; the gallery post is not a substitute |

## Instructor preparation

- Create a Blackboard gallery that accepts text, audio, video, and links.
- Make posts private to the class and disable public indexing.
- Post one model response and one deliberately vague response for comparison.
- Reserve a campus-computer completion period before the deadline.
- Publish a backup submission route and the oral-reflection make-up appointments.
- Check accessibility of the gallery and course-evaluation links while signed out where possible.

## Assessment boundary

- Final Artifact Package: graded, 10%, standard rubric.
- Gallery post and peer responses: required completion activity, not graded for production quality.
- Transfer reflection: formative.
- Week 13 oral reflection: separate graded assessment; never replaced by an asynchronous recording or gallery post.
