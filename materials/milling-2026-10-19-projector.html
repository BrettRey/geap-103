Talk to your classmates

GEAP 103 · October 19

  1. 1What language will your app teach, or what's your own project?Ask more: Who would want to use it?
  2. 2Who is it for?Ask more: Where will they use it, and what do they need to say or do?
  3. 3Which situation would you teach first?Ask more: Why that one?
  4. 4Which apps have you used to learn a language?Ask more: What did you like, and what was annoying?
  5. 5When you get an answer wrong, what should an app do?Ask more: What would make you want to try again?
  6. 6What could go wrong if AI writes phrases in your language?Ask more: How would you check them?
  7. 7Would you like to work alone or in a group?Ask more: What could you do well in a group?
  8. 8Next week is Reading Week, so there's no class. What will you do?Ask more: Will you work on your app?
