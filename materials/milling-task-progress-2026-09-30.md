# GEAP 103 milling questions, September 30, 2026

1. Which task did you work on last week, and where did you stop? Ask more: What will you do first today?
2. What did the agent make for you? Was it right? Ask more: How did you check it? If it hasn't made anything yet, what will you ask it to make first?
3. What didn't work the way you expected? Ask more: What did you try next? Who or what helped?
4. Did the agent ask your permission to do something? What did you decide? Ask more: What did you want to know before deciding? If it hasn't asked yet, what would you want to know?
5. You ask the agent to change a file. Then you want the old text back. What do you do? Ask more: How do you know you're looking at the old version?
6. What's one thing you did last week? Explain it so a friend who wasn't there could understand. Ask more: Which word was hard to find in English?
7. If you started a project of your own today, what small piece would you make first? Ask more: Who would use it?
8. What could you help a classmate with today? What do you need help with? Ask more: How would you describe the problem?
