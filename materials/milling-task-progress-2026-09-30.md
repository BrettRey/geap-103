# Where are you now?

GEAP 103 | September 30, 2026 | Speaking warm-up

## Questions

### 1. Where I stopped

Which task did you work on last week, and where did you stop?

**Ask more:** What will you do first today?

### 2. Something the agent made

What did the agent make for you? Was it right?

**Ask more:** How did you check it? If it hasn't made anything yet, what will you ask it to make first?

### 3. Something that didn't work

What didn't work the way you expected?

**Ask more:** What did you try next? Who or what helped?

### 4. Yes or no

Did the agent ask your permission to do something? What did you decide?

**Ask more:** What did you want to know before deciding? If it hasn't asked yet, what would you want to know?

### 5. Getting the old version back

You ask the agent to change a file. Then you want the old text back. What do you do?

**Ask more:** How do you know you're looking at the old version?

### 6. Explain it to a friend

What's one thing you did last week? Explain it so a friend who wasn't there could understand.

**Ask more:** Which word was hard to find in English?

### 7. Something of your own

If you started a project of your own today, what small piece would you make first?

**Ask more:** Who would use it?

### 8. Help

What could you help a classmate with today? What do you need help with?

**Ask more:** How would you describe the problem?
