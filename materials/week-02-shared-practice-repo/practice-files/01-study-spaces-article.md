# Campus Study Spaces for New Students

Many new students want a quiet place to read, write, or meet a partner. Humber students can use different study spaces for different purposes.

The **North Library quiet zone** is open **Monday to Thursday from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.** Phone calls are not allowed there.

The **Lakeshore Learning Commons** has group tables and power outlets. After **3:00 p.m.**, students must **book a table in advance**.

Students can **borrow a laptop charger for up to 4 hours** if they show a student card at the service desk.

Food is allowed **only in the cafe area**, not in the quiet study spaces.

The **peer tutoring desk** is open **Tuesday and Wednesday from 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.**
