# GEAP 103 compressed Weeks 9–10: instructor materials

Use with [`lesson-plans/compressed-weeks-09-10.md`](../lesson-plans/compressed-weeks-09-10.md). The lesson plan governs if details conflict.

## Before the previous class ends

Display:

> Choose an artifact you already started. Write three ordered tasks for improving it. Identify one task you must finish, one you can reduce, and one you can omit if necessary. This is Goal Document entry 9.

Have starter artifacts available for students without a usable draft. Students without reliable home access complete the task list and copy the needed files before leaving.

For an AI evaluation / safety project, use the [student guide](AI_Evaluation_Safety_Project_Guide.md). The test record and result are the artifact.

## Slides and handouts

### Slide 1: readiness

> Open your artifact and Goal Document task list.
>
> No artifact? Choose a starter and give it a real audience or purpose.
>
> Account problem? Tell me the exact blocked step. Start on the local or browser path I give you.

### Slide 2: vocabulary task

Terms: **design, draft, evaluate, requirement, focus**

Rewrite:

> Make my project better. Add some things. Fix the design.

Turn it into three specific, ordered tasks. Use at least three terms.

### Slide 3: process table

| What I wanted | What the tool/AI did | What I changed | How I checked |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |

### Slide 4: peer/client check

**Presenter:** What did you intend? What does the draft do? What is the biggest gap? What decision did you make?

**Partner/client:** Ask one genuine question. Then write:

> I understood _____. I am not sure about _____. Does your requirement mean _____?

Switch roles. Save a screenshot or a dated, signed two-sentence note.

### Slide 5: verification note

> I checked _____. I found _____. I fixed _____. I did not finish _____ because _____.

### Slide 6: Goal Document entry 10

> Compare what you planned with what you built. What is the biggest gap, and why did it happen? If you had more time, what would you change?

### Slide 7: collaboration inventory

> I am interested in _____.  
> I can contribute _____.  
> I need help with _____.

Identify two possible collaborators. Groups are finalized next meeting.

### Slide 8: package checklist

- Artifact
- Process documentation in your own words
- Verification note
- Peer-sharing evidence
- Exact section deadline

## Stop rules

- At 1:10, stop building even if the artifact is unfinished; protect peer explanation.
- At 2:20, stop technical repair; protect the Goal Document reflection.
- Do not troubleshoot an individualized Education-verification decision in front of the class.
- Do not finalize groups by excluding an absent student.
- Do not require home work from a student who has already reported no dependable home device or connection.
