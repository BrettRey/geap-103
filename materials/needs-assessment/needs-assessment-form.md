## Teacher settings

- Use a Humber Microsoft Forms account with Humber-only sign-in.
- Turn on **Record name** and **One response per person**. This form is identifiable, not anonymous.
- Leave every question optional and question shuffling off.
- Don't add co-authors or create a results-sharing link.
- Leave **Accept responses** off until the applicable collection notice is added and approved use is confirmed.
- Share the response link through Blackboard. Never share an editing or results link with students.
- Keep identifying responses and exports in Humber systems, out of this public repository and external LLMs.
- If a student can't sign in, offer a private conversation or a paper copy handed directly to the teacher.

Use the answers to plan access support, short demonstrations, and project exploration. Treat skill answers as self-reports, not proof of what students can or can't do. Share broad class patterns only when individuals can't be identified.
