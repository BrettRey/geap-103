# How you'll be assessed from now on

GEAP 103 | October 2026

You're working through the tasks at your own pace, so everyone is at a different point. We're changing the assessment to fit that.

**These won't happen:** the File System package, the Micro-Defences in groups of three, the Git Episodes, and the separate written artifacts. Nothing you've already done is lost.

**Your grade has four parts.**

| Part | Weight | What it is |
|---|---:|---|
| Conferences | 40% | Short one-to-one talks with your teacher in class, while everyone works. You show something you did and explain it, including what you asked the AI and what you kept. The last one replaces the oral reflection. |
| Decision Log | 25% | Your eight best entries, as before. |
| In-class writing | 15% | Three 10-minute writing tasks in class, by hand, without AI. Your best two count. |
| Final package | 20% | Your finished product, with short instructions, what works and what doesn't, and a note in your own words about how you made it. |

**In your conferences, show these three things.** You don't need to show them all at once.

- How you got back an earlier version of a file.
- How you solved a technical problem, or described it clearly enough to get help.
- A change you made to a classmate's work, the comment you got, and your reply.

**Levels.** For each course outcome, your teacher notes how well you showed it:

- **4:** you do it yourself and explain it.
- **3:** you do it yourself, but your explanation isn't complete.
- **2:** you do part of it, or you need help.
- **1:** you try, but there's little to show.
- **0:** not shown yet.

Levels 3 and 4 count when you show them twice, and at least once without AI: in a conference or in the in-class writing. One showing without AI is enough for level 2. Your best level counts, so a weak first conference won't pull your grade down.

**To pass, show every course outcome at level 2 or higher.** If one is missing near the end, your teacher will give you a chance to show it.

Dates are in your section's schedule.
