## Combined November 23 class

Before leaving the November 16 class, choose an existing artifact to develop and write a specific task list in Goal Document entry 9. On November 23, you will build, explain the draft to a peer, verify it, write Goal Document entry 10, and begin finding collaborators. Students who cannot work at home receive time to finish and submit using a campus computer.

## Asynchronous closeout

By December 18:

1. Submit the final package.
2. Post a short explanation of your artifact to the class gallery. Audio, video, or text is accepted.
3. Respond specifically to two classmates.
4. Complete the transfer reflection and institutional course evaluation.

If home technology or media access is a barrier, complete this work on December 14 or arrange campus-lab access. Production polish in the gallery post is not graded.

## Technology access

GitHub Education requires your own current student documentation, and Copilot Student activation is separate. Begin early, but do not send documentation to your instructor. If GitHub, Copilot, OneDrive, or software installation is unavailable, tell the instructor which step is blocked. The course provides an equivalent path; your grade does not depend on receiving a particular account benefit.

## If you miss an assessment class

Contact the instructor. Your best two of the three in-class writing tasks count, so one missed task doesn't lower your grade. If you miss your final conference, the make-up must be completed by December 18 and is a live conversation with the instructor.
