# GEAP 103 — 13-content-meeting delivery map

**Audience:** Instructors who have 13 meetings available for GEAP 103 content  
**Use case:** A separate orientation/general meeting followed by the course sequence

This map preserves Assessment Framework v6. It combines curricular Weeks 9 and 10. It does not combine Weeks 12 and 13, because Week 12 supplies the evidence mapping and rehearsal required for the Week 13 oral reflection.

| Content meeting | Curricular unit | Assessment / modification |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Week 1 | Standard plan |
| 2 | Week 2 | Standard plan; Decision Logs begin |
| 3 | Week 3 | Standard plan with three access paths |
| 4 | Week 4 | Micro-Defence 1 + File System package |
| 5 | Week 5 | Standard plan |
| 6 | Week 6 | DL feedback round 1; Git Episodes begin |
| 7 | Week 7 | Standard plan with GitHub/equivalent collaboration paths |
| 8 | Week 8 | Micro-Defence 2; collect DL entries 3–5 |

| 12 | Week 13 | Four-minute oral reflection; collaborative submission |
| 13 | Week 14 | Final Artifact Package + sharing |

## Compression instructions

At the end of meeting 8, students choose an existing artifact to develop and write Goal Document entry 9 as a task list. Students without dependable home technology complete this before leaving.

Use [`compressed-weeks-09-10.md`](../lesson-plans/compressed-weeks-09-10.md) for meeting 9. Protect:

- meaningful building time;
- a peer check-in that creates sharing evidence;
- verification and process documentation;
- Goal Document entry 10; and
- an initial interest inventory for collaborative grouping.

The Mid-Semester Artifact Package may be due at the end of the day or within 48 hours, but the extension must include a campus-computer option. Finalize groups in the first 10–15 minutes of meeting 10 so an absence in the compressed class does not strand a student.

## What not to compress

- Do not merge Weeks 1–3: they establish the files → prompting → version-history sequence.
- Do not merge Weeks 4, 8, or 11 with another assessment unit.
- Do not merge Weeks 12 and 13: the four-minute reflection assumes that evidence is already mapped and open.
- Do not make unverified GitHub Education or Copilot access a prerequisite for moving forward.
