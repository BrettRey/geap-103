## What could go wrong

- **GitHub sign-in:** some students may be asked to set up two-factor authentication when they sign in. It needs a phone app and some minutes, so put them on the help list rather than the conference list.
- **"Use this template" isn't there:** the student isn't in the class group or isn't signed in. The check is on slide 1.
- **An invitation link says there's no invitation:** it went to a different account, or the student hasn't been invited. Ask for the username again.
- **Copilot wants an update:** not in class (slide 1).
